About me

I work across the full analytics loop: framing a question, modeling data in SQL and dbt, analyzing results in Python, and communicating the answer. As an embedded analytics partner for product teams, I define metrics, design and analyze A/B tests and causal studies, and build the KPI dashboards and funnel, cohort, and retention analyses that guide the roadmap.

My earlier work in healthcare shaped how I approach data. I analyzed Medicare Part C and D claims to detect fraud, waste, and abuse, and I ran cohort analyses on public health data. I hold an MPH in Epidemiology and Biostatistics, and I now build LLM applications, including natural language query interfaces and agentic workflows.
