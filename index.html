About me

I’m interested in the space between data, technology, and decision-making. My work is about taking complex problems, figuring out what the data can actually tell us, and turning that into something useful.

Since joining Kooth in 2020, I’ve grown into a Data Analyst/Scientist on the Analytics team. I’ve worked across Product, Marketing, Service Delivery, and Clinical, partnering with teams to understand user behavior, measure product performance, evaluate new ideas, and make better decisions. I enjoy being involved in the entire process, from figuring out the right question to building the data foundations, doing the analysis, and communicating what it means.

My background brings together three areas I truly enjoy: product analytics and experimentation, statistical analysis and data science, and building reliable data infrastructure. That means everything from designing and evaluating experiments to developing reusable data models, exploring behavioral patterns, and applying statistical methods to real-world problems. More recently, I’ve been exploring how LLMs and agentic workflows can make data more accessible and help automate parts of the analytical process.

Before moving into product analytics, I worked in healthcare analytics and public health research, including Medicare claims analysis at Qlarant and research at Cook County Health. I also hold an MPH in Epidemiology and Biostatistics. That background has shaped how I approach analytical problems: with an emphasis on good study design, understanding bias, and being thoughtful about what the data can and cannot tell us.

Ultimately, I enjoy working on problems where good analysis can change how a product works, how a team operates, or how a decision gets made.
